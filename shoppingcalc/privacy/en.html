Privacy Policy

App: Okaimono Calculator (お買い物計算機) / Provider: Tons Lab
Effective date: October 22, 2026

This policy explains how Tons Lab ("we") handles information in the Android app "Okaimono Calculator" ("the app").

1. About the app

The app calculates discounted prices, compares unit prices, and compares real prices after loyalty points. No account or sign-in is required.

2. Information we collect

We do not collect personally identifying information such as your name, address, phone number, email address, location, contacts, or photos.

The values you enter (prices, quantities, points, notes) and the calculation results are not sent off your device. They never reach our servers and we cannot see them.

3. Information stored on your device

The app stores your calculation history (automatic history and results you choose to save) only on your device. You can delete it in the app (delete or clear history) or by uninstalling the app. This data is excluded from device backups.

4. Information collected by third-party services

To understand usage and to show ads, the app uses the following services provided by Google. They may automatically collect the information below from your device.

Google handles this information under its own policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services
  • Privacy and Security in Firebase

5. How we use information

  • To improve the app and find problems
  • To analyze usage statistically
  • To show ads that help cover the cost of running the app

6. Ad personalization and opting out

You can turn off ad personalization or reset your advertising ID in your device settings (Android Settings > Google > Ads). Uninstalling the app stops all collection by the app.

7. Sharing with third parties

We do not provide your information to third parties without your consent, except where required by law. As described in section 4, Google's services collect information for their own purposes.

8. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

9. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the information handled by the app against unauthorized access, disclosure, and alteration.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when necessary. Material changes will be announced on this page. Using the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.
